Corresponding source for GPL components used by STG Phantom VPN
================================================================

By default STG Phantom VPN does not contain any GPL binary. When OpenVPN is missing, the app
downloads the unmodified official installer from build.openvpn.net over HTTPS, checks its
SHA-256 and Authenticode signature (OpenVPN Inc.), and asks Windows (UAC) to install it.

Installer used:  OpenVPN-2.6.23-I002-<arch>.msi
